# Operation Don’t Stress Over Dinner — Recipe Arsenal

220 recipes across 11 categories. Every recipe serves **three people**, including any accompaniment listed. Quantities are starting points; season to taste.

Weights are uncooked unless stated otherwise. One can of tomatoes is 400 g; one can of beans or chickpeas is approximately 240 g drained. Drain and rinse canned pulses. Stock means prepared liquid stock. Chicken thighs are boneless and skinless; shrimp are raw, peeled and deveined. Thaw frozen seafood before cooking. Oven temperatures are for conventional ovens. Cooking times vary with thickness: check that poultry is cooked through and braising meat is tender. Use vegetarian stock and cheese in vegetarian dishes.

## Dutch oven dishes

### 1. Beef Bourguignon
**Ingredients:** 600 g stewing beef, cubed; 1 onion, chopped; 2 carrots, sliced; 250 g mushrooms, quartered; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 250 ml red wine; 300 ml beef stock; 1 tbsp tomato paste; 1 tsp thyme; 1 tbsp olive oil; 450 g potatoes; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Heat oil in a Dutch oven and brown beef in batches; remove. Cook onion and carrots for 6 minutes, add garlic and tomato paste for 1 minute, then wine, stock, thyme and beef. Cover and bake at 160°C for 2–3 hours, until tender; add mushrooms for the final 40 minutes. Season and serve with boiled potatoes.

### 2. Chicken Cacciatore
**Ingredients:** 550 g chicken thighs; 1 onion, sliced; 2 bell peppers, sliced; 3 garlic cloves, chopped; 1 can chopped tomatoes; 100 ml red wine; 1 tsp oregano; 1 tbsp olive oil; 210 g wholewheat pasta; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Brown chicken in oil and remove. Soften onion and peppers for 7 minutes; add garlic, oregano, wine, tomatoes and chicken. Cover and bake at 175°C for 50–60 minutes, until chicken is cooked and tender; uncover for the final 15 minutes to thicken. Season and serve with pasta cooked according to its packet.

### 3. Moroccan Chicken and Chickpea Stew
**Ingredients:** 500 g chicken thighs; 1 can chickpeas; 1 onion and 2 carrots, chopped; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 1 can tomatoes; 200 ml chicken stock; 1 tsp each cumin, ground coriander and paprika; ¼ tsp cinnamon; ½ lemon; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Brown chicken in oil and remove. Soften onion and carrots for 6 minutes, then stir in garlic and spices for 1 minute. Add tomatoes, stock, chickpeas and chicken. Cover and bake at 175°C for 55–65 minutes, until chicken is tender. Finish with lemon juice and seasoning.

### 4. Pork, White Bean and Tomato Stew
**Ingredients:** 650 g pork shoulder, trimmed and cut into 3 cm cubes; 1 can cannellini beans; 1 onion and 2 carrots, chopped; 3 garlic cloves, chopped; 1 can tomatoes; 250 ml chicken stock; 1 tsp each rosemary and thyme; 1 tbsp olive oil; 1 tsp red-wine vinegar; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Brown pork in oil in batches and remove. Soften onion and carrots, add garlic and herbs, then pork, tomatoes and stock. Cover and bake at 160°C for 2–3 hours, until fork-tender; add beans for the final 25 minutes. Season and finish with vinegar. For a smoked version, smoke the seasoned pork in large chunks at 107°C for 45–60 minutes, then cube and immediately begin the braise.

### 5. Turkey and Black Bean Chili
**Ingredients:** 450 g turkey mince; 1 can black beans; 1 onion and 1 bell pepper, chopped; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 1 can tomatoes; 200 ml chicken stock; 1 tsp each cumin, smoked paprika and oregano; ¼–½ tsp chili flakes; 1 tbsp olive oil; ½ lime; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Brown turkey in oil, breaking it up. Add onion and pepper and cook for 6 minutes; add garlic and spices for 1 minute. Stir in tomatoes, beans and stock, then simmer gently, partly covered, for 40–50 minutes. Add lime juice and season; uncover towards the end if too loose.

### 6. French Lentil, Sausage and Vegetable Stew
**Ingredients:** 180 g green lentils, rinsed; 240 g lean pork sausages; 1 onion, 2 carrots and 2 celery stalks, chopped; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 700 ml chicken stock; 1 tsp thyme; 1 bay leaf; 2 tsp Dijon mustard; 1 tsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Brown sausages in oil, then remove. Cook vegetables for 7 minutes; add garlic, lentils, stock, thyme and bay. Simmer covered for 25 minutes. Return sausages and simmer another 20–25 minutes, until sausages are cooked and lentils tender, adding water as needed. Slice sausages, return to the pot, and stir in mustard and seasoning.

### 7. Italian Beef, Tomato and Cannellini Stew
**Ingredients:** 600 g lean stewing beef, cubed; 1 can cannellini beans; 1 onion and 2 carrots, chopped; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 1 can tomatoes; 250 ml beef stock; 1 tsp each rosemary and oregano; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Brown beef in oil in batches and remove. Soften onion and carrots for 6 minutes, then add garlic and herbs. Return beef with tomatoes and stock. Cover and bake at 160°C for 2–3 hours, until beef is tender. Add beans for the final 25 minutes and season.

### 8. Lemon Chicken, White Bean and Spinach Stew
**Ingredients:** 500 g chicken thighs; 1 can cannellini beans; 1 onion and 2 carrots, chopped; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 500 ml chicken stock; 1 lemon; 1 tsp thyme; 150 g spinach; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Brown chicken in oil and remove. Soften onion and carrots, then add garlic, thyme, stock and chicken. Cover and bake at 175°C for 50 minutes; add beans and bake for 10 minutes more. Break up chicken, stir in spinach until wilted, and add lemon zest, lemon juice to taste, and seasoning.

### 9. Beef and Barley Stew
**Ingredients:** 550 g stewing beef, cubed; 100 g pearl barley; 1 onion, 2 carrots and 2 celery stalks, chopped; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 800 ml beef stock; 1 tbsp tomato paste; 1 tsp thyme; 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Brown beef in oil and remove. Soften vegetables for 7 minutes, add garlic and tomato paste, then beef, stock, thyme and Worcestershire. Cover and bake at 160°C for 2 hours. Add barley and cook another 40–60 minutes, until both beef and barley are tender, topping up with hot water if needed. Season.

### 10. Red Lentil, Sweet Potato and Coconut Curry
**Ingredients:** 180 g red lentils, rinsed; 450 g sweet potato, diced; 1 onion, chopped; 2 garlic cloves and 20 g ginger, minced; 200 ml light coconut milk; 1 can tomatoes; 400 ml vegetable stock; 1 tbsp curry powder; 150 g spinach; ½ lime; 1 tbsp oil; salt.

**Directions:** Soften onion in oil for 5 minutes. Add garlic, ginger and curry powder for 1 minute, then sweet potato, lentils, tomatoes, coconut milk and stock. Simmer partly covered for 25–35 minutes, stirring occasionally, until tender; add water if needed. Stir in spinach, lime juice and salt.

### 11. Greek Lemon Chicken, Potato and Olive Braise
**Ingredients:** 550 g chicken thighs; 600 g potatoes, cut into 3 cm chunks; 1 onion, sliced; 3 garlic cloves, chopped; 300 ml chicken stock; 1 lemon; 1 tsp oregano; 60 g pitted olives; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Brown chicken in oil and remove. Soften onion, then add garlic, potatoes, stock, lemon zest, half the lemon juice and oregano. Return chicken, cover and bake at 175°C for 50–60 minutes, until potatoes and chicken are tender. Add olives and bake uncovered for 10 minutes. Season and add remaining lemon juice to taste.

### 12. Tuscan White Bean, Kale and Sausage Stew
**Ingredients:** 220 g Italian-style pork sausage, casing removed; 2 cans cannellini beans; 1 onion and 2 carrots, chopped; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 650 ml chicken stock; 200 g kale, tough stems removed and leaves chopped; 1 tsp rosemary; 1 tsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Brown crumbled sausage in oil until cooked; remove. Soften onion and carrots for 7 minutes, add garlic and rosemary, then beans and stock. Simmer for 20 minutes. Return sausage with kale and simmer for 10–15 minutes, until kale is tender. Mash a few beans against the pot to thicken and season.

### 13. Paprika Chicken and Mushroom Stew
**Ingredients:** 550 g chicken thighs; 300 g mushrooms, sliced; 1 onion, sliced; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 1 tbsp sweet paprika; 1 tbsp tomato paste; 300 ml chicken stock; 100 g plain Greek yogurt; 180 g egg noodles; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Brown chicken in oil and remove. Brown mushrooms, then soften onion. Stir in garlic, paprika and tomato paste for 30 seconds; add stock and chicken. Cover and bake at 175°C for 50–60 minutes. Cook noodles separately. Take stew off the heat, temper yogurt with a little hot sauce, stir it in, season and serve over noodles.

### 14. Beef, Cabbage and Potato Pot
**Ingredients:** 550 g lean stewing beef, cubed; 400 g green cabbage, sliced; 450 g potatoes, chunked; 2 carrots and 1 onion, chopped; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 550 ml beef stock; 1 tsp thyme; 1 bay leaf; 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Brown beef in oil. Add onion and carrots for 6 minutes, then garlic, stock, thyme, bay and Worcestershire. Cover and bake at 160°C for 90 minutes. Add potatoes and cabbage and bake another 60–90 minutes, until beef and potatoes are tender. Remove bay and season.

### 15. Spanish Chicken, Pepper and Butter Bean Stew
**Ingredients:** 500 g chicken thighs; 1 can butter beans; 2 red peppers and 1 onion, sliced; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 1 can tomatoes; 150 ml chicken stock; 1 tsp smoked paprika; 1 tbsp olive oil; 1 tbsp red-wine vinegar; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Brown chicken in oil and remove. Soften peppers and onion for 7 minutes, then stir in garlic and paprika. Add tomatoes, stock and chicken. Cover and bake at 175°C for 45 minutes. Add beans and cook uncovered for 15 minutes, until chicken is tender. Finish with vinegar and seasoning.

### 16. Turkey Meatballs in Tomato and Courgette Sauce
**Ingredients:** 500 g turkey mince; 1 egg; 35 g breadcrumbs; 1 tsp oregano; 1 onion and 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 1 courgette, diced; 1 can tomatoes; 150 ml water; 1 tbsp olive oil; 180 g wholewheat pasta; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Mix turkey, egg, breadcrumbs, half the oregano, salt and pepper; form 12 meatballs. Soften onion and courgette in oil for 7 minutes, add garlic, tomatoes, water and remaining oregano. Nestle meatballs into sauce; cover and simmer gently for 20–25 minutes, turning once, until cooked through. Serve with separately cooked pasta.

### 17. Aubergine, Chickpea and Tomato Braise
**Ingredients:** 2 aubergines, cubed; 2 cans chickpeas; 1 onion, chopped; 3 garlic cloves, chopped; 1 can tomatoes; 150 ml vegetable stock; 1 tsp each cumin and smoked paprika; 2 tbsp olive oil; ½ lemon; 100 g plain yogurt; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Brown aubergine in oil in two batches; remove. Soften onion, add garlic and spices, then aubergine, tomatoes, stock and chickpeas. Cover and bake at 180°C for 40–50 minutes, until aubergine is silky. Season and finish with lemon juice. Spoon yogurt onto each serving.

### 18. Mushroom and White Bean Pot
**Ingredients:** 500 g mushrooms, quartered; 2 cans cannellini beans; 1 onion, chopped; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 300 ml vegetable stock; 1 tsp thyme; 2 tsp Dijon mustard; 100 g spinach; 1 tbsp olive oil; 180 g wholegrain bread; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Brown mushrooms in oil in batches and remove. Soften onion, add garlic and thyme, then stock, beans and mushrooms. Simmer partly covered for 20 minutes. Mash a few beans to thicken, stir in mustard and spinach, and cook for 2 minutes. Season and serve with bread.

### 19. Pork, Apple and Mustard Braise
**Ingredients:** 600 g pork shoulder, trimmed and cubed; 1 onion, sliced; 2 carrots, chunked; 2 apples, cored and wedged; 300 ml chicken stock; 1 tsp thyme; 1 tbsp Dijon mustard; 1 tbsp olive oil; 450 g potatoes; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Brown pork in oil and remove. Soften onion and carrots, then add pork, stock and thyme. Cover and bake at 160°C for 2–3 hours, until fork-tender, adding apples for the final 30 minutes. Stir in mustard and season. Serve with boiled potatoes.

### 20. Chickpea, Spinach and Apricot Tagine-Style Stew
**Ingredients:** 2 cans chickpeas; 1 onion and 2 carrots, chopped; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 1 can tomatoes; 250 ml vegetable stock; 60 g dried apricots, chopped; 1 tsp each cumin and ground coriander; ¼ tsp cinnamon; 150 g spinach; 150 g couscous; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Soften onion and carrots in oil for 7 minutes, then add garlic and spices for 1 minute. Add tomatoes, stock, chickpeas and apricots. Cover and simmer for 25 minutes; stir in spinach and season. Prepare couscous according to its packet and serve beneath the stew.

## Pastas

### 1. Lemon, Pea and Ricotta Pasta
**Ingredients:** 240 g pasta; 250 g frozen peas; 150 g ricotta; 1 lemon; 2 garlic cloves, sliced; 1 tbsp olive oil; 20 g Parmesan; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Cook pasta, adding peas for its final 3 minutes; reserve a mug of cooking water. Gently cook garlic in oil for 1 minute. Add drained pasta and peas, ricotta, lemon zest and a splash of cooking water. Stir off the heat until creamy; add lemon juice, Parmesan and seasoning.

### 2. Tuna, Tomato and Caper Spaghetti
**Ingredients:** 240 g spaghetti; 2 cans tuna in water, drained, approximately 250 g total; 1 can tomatoes; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 1 tbsp capers, drained; 1 tbsp olive oil; ¼ tsp chili flakes; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Cook spaghetti. Meanwhile, soften garlic and chili in oil for 30 seconds; add tomatoes and simmer for 12 minutes. Fold in tuna and capers and warm for 2 minutes. Toss with drained spaghetti and a little pasta water; season.

### 3. Mushroom and Spinach Tagliatelle
**Ingredients:** 240 g tagliatelle; 400 g mushrooms, sliced; 150 g spinach; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 100 g light cream cheese; 1 tbsp olive oil; 25 g Parmesan; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Cook tagliatelle, reserving some cooking water. Brown mushrooms in oil for 8–10 minutes. Add garlic for 30 seconds, then spinach until wilted. Stir in cream cheese with enough pasta water to make a sauce. Toss with pasta, Parmesan and seasoning.

### 4. Roasted Pepper and White Bean Pasta
**Ingredients:** 240 g pasta; 250 g roasted peppers from a jar, drained; 1 can cannellini beans; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 1 tbsp olive oil; ½ tsp smoked paprika; ½ lemon; 20 g Parmesan; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Cook pasta and reserve cooking water. Gently fry garlic and paprika in oil for 30 seconds. Blend peppers, beans and 100 ml pasta water until smooth; add to the pan and heat through. Toss with pasta, adding water as needed. Finish with lemon juice, Parmesan and seasoning.

### 5. Broccoli, Lemon and Almond Pasta
**Ingredients:** 240 g wholewheat pasta; 450 g broccoli, small florets and diced stalk; 2 garlic cloves, sliced; 1 lemon; 30 g almonds, chopped; 30 g Parmesan; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Cook pasta, adding broccoli for its final 4 minutes. Toast almonds in a dry pan and remove. Cook garlic gently in oil, then add drained pasta and broccoli with a splash of pasta water. Mash some broccoli into the sauce. Finish with lemon zest, juice, Parmesan, almonds and seasoning.

### 6. Courgette, Basil and Feta Fusilli
**Ingredients:** 240 g fusilli; 2 courgettes, diced; 250 g cherry tomatoes, halved; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 90 g feta; 15 g basil; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Cook fusilli. Brown courgettes in oil for 7–8 minutes; add garlic and tomatoes and cook for 4 minutes. Toss with pasta and a splash of its water. Crumble in feta, tear in basil and season, allowing the cheese to soften into the sauce.

### 7. Turkey and Lentil Bolognese
**Ingredients:** 240 g spaghetti; 300 g turkey mince; 1 can lentils, drained; 1 onion and 1 carrot, finely chopped; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 400 ml passata; 1 tsp oregano; 1 tbsp olive oil; 20 g Parmesan; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Soften onion and carrot in oil for 7 minutes. Add turkey and brown, then garlic, oregano, lentils and passata. Simmer for 25 minutes, adding water if needed, and season. Cook spaghetti and serve with sauce and Parmesan.

### 8. Tomato, Olive and Mozzarella Pasta Bake
**Ingredients:** 240 g penne; 400 ml passata; 1 courgette, diced; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 50 g olives, sliced; 125 g mozzarella, drained and torn; 1 tsp oregano; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Cook penne 2 minutes short of its packet time. Soften courgette in oil, add garlic, passata, olives and oregano, and simmer for 8 minutes. Season and mix with pasta. Transfer to a baking dish, top with mozzarella and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes, until bubbling.

### 9. Chicken, Spinach and Pesto Pasta
**Ingredients:** 240 g pasta; 350 g chicken breast, sliced; 150 g spinach; 250 g cherry tomatoes, halved; 40 g basil pesto; ½ lemon; 1 tsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Cook pasta and reserve cooking water. Cook chicken in oil for 6–8 minutes, until cooked through. Add tomatoes for 3 minutes and spinach until wilted. Toss with pasta, pesto and a splash of pasta water off the heat. Finish with lemon juice and seasoning.

### 10. Sardine, Tomato and Parsley Spaghetti
**Ingredients:** 240 g spaghetti; 2 cans sardines, approximately 220 g drained; 1 can tomatoes; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 1 lemon; 15 g parsley, chopped; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Cook spaghetti. Gently fry garlic in oil for 30 seconds, add tomatoes and simmer for 12 minutes. Fold in sardines, breaking them into large pieces, and warm through. Toss with pasta, parsley, lemon zest and lemon juice to taste. Season.

### 11. Aubergine and Tomato Rigatoni
**Ingredients:** 240 g rigatoni; 1 large aubergine, cubed; 1 can tomatoes; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 1 tsp oregano; 15 g basil; 30 g Parmesan; 1½ tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Toss aubergine with oil and roast at 220°C for 25–30 minutes, turning once. Meanwhile, simmer tomatoes, garlic and oregano for 15 minutes and cook pasta. Stir aubergine into sauce, season and toss with pasta. Finish with basil and Parmesan.

### 12. Spinach and Ricotta Cannelloni
**Ingredients:** 180 g cannelloni tubes; 300 g frozen spinach, thawed and squeezed dry; 250 g ricotta; 500 ml passata; 1 garlic clove, grated; 40 g Parmesan; ¼ tsp nutmeg; salt and pepper.

Spread some sauce in a dish, arrange tubes, cover completely with remaining sauce and sprinkle with remaining cheese. Cover with foil and bake at 190°C for 35 minutes; uncover for 10–15 minutes, until pasta is tender.

### 13. Sausage, Fennel and Tomato Pasta

### 14. Pea, Ham and Mustard Pasta

**Directions:** Cook pasta, adding peas for the final 3 minutes, and reserve cooking water. Warm cream cheese, mustard and 100 ml pasta water gently in a large pan. Add ham, pasta and peas and heat through. Finish with lemon juice, chives and seasoning.

### 15. Garlic, Tomato and Chickpea Orzo
**Ingredients:** 210 g orzo; 1 can chickpeas; 300 g cherry tomatoes, halved; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 550 ml vegetable stock; 100 g spinach; ½ lemon; 1 tbsp olive oil; 25 g Parmesan; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Soften garlic in oil briefly, then add tomatoes for 4 minutes. Stir in orzo, chickpeas and stock. Simmer uncovered for 10–15 minutes, stirring often and adding water if needed, until orzo is tender. Wilt in spinach and finish with lemon, Parmesan and seasoning.

### 16. Roasted Cauliflower and Paprika Pasta
**Ingredients:** 240 g pasta; 500 g cauliflower, small florets; 1 tsp smoked paprika; 2 garlic cloves, grated; 150 g plain Greek yogurt; ½ lemon; 1½ tbsp olive oil; 25 g Parmesan; salt and pepper.

Off the heat, toss pasta and cauliflower with yogurt mixture, loosening with warm pasta water. Add lemon juice and pepper; warm gently without boiling.

### 17. Green Bean and Walnut Pesto Pasta
**Ingredients:** 240 g wholewheat pasta; 350 g green beans, trimmed; 25 g basil; 30 g walnuts; 30 g Parmesan; 1 small garlic clove; 1 tbsp olive oil; ½ lemon; salt and pepper.

### 18. Lentil and Mushroom Lasagne
**Ingredients:** 180 g lasagne sheets; 1 can lentils, drained; 300 g mushrooms, chopped; 1 onion and 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 500 ml passata; 200 g ricotta; 200 ml milk; 40 g Parmesan; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

Layer sauce, sheets and ricotta mixture in a dish, finishing with ricotta and Parmesan. Cover and bake at 190°C for 30 minutes; uncover for 15–20 minutes, until pasta is tender.

### 19. Butter Bean, Rosemary and Tomato Pasta
**Ingredients:** 240 g pasta; 1 can butter beans; 1 can tomatoes; 1 onion, chopped; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 1 tsp rosemary; 1 tbsp olive oil; ½ lemon; 20 g Parmesan; salt and pepper.

### 20. Roasted Tomato and Cottage Cheese Pasta
**Ingredients:** 240 g pasta; 500 g cherry tomatoes; 200 g cottage cheese; 2 garlic cloves, peeled; 15 g basil; 1 tbsp olive oil; 20 g Parmesan; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Toss tomatoes and garlic with oil and seasoning; roast at 200°C for 25 minutes. Cook pasta. Blend cottage cheese with roasted garlic, half the tomatoes and a splash of pasta water. Toss pasta with sauce and remaining tomatoes over gentle heat. Finish with basil and Parmesan.

## Soups

### 1. Carrot, Ginger and Garlic Soup
**Ingredients:** 650 g carrots, sliced; 1 onion, chopped; 3 garlic cloves and 25 g ginger, chopped; 700 ml vegetable stock; 100 ml light coconut milk; ½ tsp cumin; 1 tbsp olive oil; ½ lemon; 240 g wholegrain bread; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Soften onion and carrots in oil for 7 minutes. Add garlic, ginger and cumin for 1 minute, then stock. Cover and simmer for 20–25 minutes, until carrots are very soft. Blend smooth, stir in coconut milk and warm through. Add lemon juice and seasoning; serve with bread.

### 2. Tomato and Red Lentil Soup
**Ingredients:** 160 g red lentils, rinsed; 1 can tomatoes; 1 onion and 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 700 ml vegetable stock; 1 tsp smoked paprika; 1 tbsp olive oil; 90 g plain yogurt; 180 g wholegrain bread; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Soften onion in oil for 5 minutes; add garlic and paprika for 30 seconds. Add tomatoes, lentils and stock and simmer for 20–25 minutes, until lentils soften. Blend or leave chunky, adjusting with water. Season and serve with a spoonful of yogurt and bread.

### 3. Chicken, Lemon and Rice Soup
**Ingredients:** 400 g chicken breast; 100 g rice; 2 carrots and 1 onion, chopped; 1 litre chicken stock; 1 lemon; 15 g dill, chopped; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Soften onion and carrots in oil for 6 minutes. Add stock, rice and chicken; simmer gently for 18–22 minutes, until chicken is cooked and rice tender. Remove chicken, shred and return. Stir in dill, lemon zest and lemon juice to taste; season.

### 4. Broccoli, White Bean and Cheddar Soup
**Ingredients:** 500 g broccoli, chopped; 1 can cannellini beans; 1 onion and 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 700 ml vegetable stock; 60 g mature Cheddar, grated; 1 tsp Dijon mustard; 1 tbsp olive oil; 180 g wholegrain bread; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Soften onion in oil, then add garlic, diced broccoli stalks, beans and stock. Simmer for 8 minutes, add florets and simmer another 7–10 minutes. Blend smooth, stir in mustard and cheese over low heat, and season. Serve with bread.

### 5. Leek, Potato and Pea Soup
**Ingredients:** 2 leeks, washed and sliced; 400 g potatoes, diced; 250 g frozen peas; 800 ml vegetable stock; 100 g plain Greek yogurt; 15 g chives, chopped; 1 tbsp olive oil; 180 g wholegrain bread; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Soften leeks in oil for 8 minutes. Add potatoes and stock and simmer for 15–20 minutes, until tender. Add peas for 3 minutes, then blend. Stir in yogurt off the heat, season and scatter with chives. Serve with bread.

### 6. Ginger Chicken and Noodle Soup
**Ingredients:** 400 g chicken breast, thinly sliced; 150 g egg noodles; 2 carrots, thinly sliced; 200 g mushrooms, sliced; 20 g ginger, grated; 2 garlic cloves, grated; 1 litre chicken stock; 1½ tbsp soy sauce; ½ lime; 3 spring onions, sliced.

**Directions:** Simmer stock, ginger, garlic, carrots and mushrooms for 8 minutes. Add chicken and simmer for 5–7 minutes, until cooked through. Cook noodles separately according to their packet and divide among bowls. Add soy sauce and lime juice to broth, ladle over noodles and top with spring onions.

### 7. Roasted Pepper and Chickpea Soup
**Ingredients:** 350 g roasted peppers from a jar, drained; 1 can chickpeas; 1 can tomatoes; 1 onion and 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 500 ml vegetable stock; 1 tsp smoked paprika; 1 tbsp olive oil; 180 g wholegrain bread; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Soften onion in oil for 6 minutes; add garlic and paprika briefly. Add peppers, chickpeas, tomatoes and stock. Simmer for 15 minutes and blend smooth, loosening with water if necessary. Season and serve with bread.

### 8. Mushroom and Pearl Barley Soup
**Ingredients:** 400 g mushrooms, sliced; 100 g pearl barley; 1 can cannellini beans; 1 onion and 2 carrots, chopped; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 1 litre vegetable stock; 1 tsp thyme; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Brown mushrooms in oil and remove. Soften onion and carrots, add garlic and thyme, then barley and stock. Simmer covered for 35–45 minutes, until barley is tender. Add beans and mushrooms for 5 minutes; thin with water if needed and season.

### 9. Sweet Potato and Peanut Soup
**Ingredients:** 500 g sweet potato, diced; 1 can chickpeas; 1 onion, chopped; 2 garlic cloves and 20 g ginger, minced; 1 can tomatoes; 600 ml vegetable stock; 40 g peanut butter; 100 g spinach; ½ lime; 1 tsp oil; salt.

### 10. White Bean and Rosemary Soup

### 12. Spinach and Tortellini Soup
