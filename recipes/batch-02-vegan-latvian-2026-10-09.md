# Op DSOD — Vegan and Latvian Recipe Arsenal

**35 easy vegan meals + 22 researched Latvian vegetarian or fish dishes. All quantities serve three people.** Accompaniments listed are included in those portions; Latvian sides and snacks are labelled in their names.

Weights are uncooked unless stated otherwise. A can of tomatoes is 400 g; a can of beans, chickpeas or lentils yields approximately 240 g drained. Drain and rinse canned pulses. Stock means prepared liquid stock. Oven temperatures are conventional. Salt, pepper and water are included where needed; season to taste.

For the vegan section, use vegetable stock, unsweetened plant-based yogurt, egg-free pasta and noodles, and vegan bread, tortillas and pesto where applicable. No dairy, eggs, honey or fish ingredients are required. These are original practical recipes, not claimed as traditional versions of the cuisines that inspire them.

The Latvian section combines traditional dishes and documented Latvian home cooking; some dishes are shared with neighbouring cuisines. Recipes are rewritten and scaled for this collection. Meat-free adaptations, simplified methods and German-supermarket substitutions are identified in the source notes. Vegetarian dishes may contain eggs or dairy; use cheese made with vegetarian rennet. Sorrel and Latvian grey peas are the main specialist ingredients, with clearly identified alternatives below. The carrot tarts and cabbage rolls take more work than the other dishes.

## Easy vegan meals

### 1. Smoky Tofu and Pepper Fajitas
**Ingredients:** 450 g firm tofu, strips; 2 peppers and 1 onion, sliced; 6 small tortillas; 1 tsp each smoked paprika and cumin; 1 lime; 120 g plant-based yogurt; 1 tbsp oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Pat tofu dry and fry in half the oil for 7–8 minutes; remove. Soften peppers and onion in remaining oil for 8 minutes. Add spices, tofu and 2 tbsp water and heat through. Finish with lime juice and seasoning; serve in warmed tortillas with yogurt.

### 2. Lemon, Lentil and Spinach Pasta
**Ingredients:** 240 g pasta; 1 can green lentils; 200 g spinach; 2 garlic cloves, sliced; 1 lemon; 30 g walnuts, chopped; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Cook pasta and reserve a mug of water. Gently fry garlic in oil for 1 minute, add lentils and 75 ml pasta water, then spinach to wilt. Toss in pasta, lemon zest and juice to taste. Season and top with walnuts.

### 3. Peanut Noodles with Tofu and Broccoli
**Ingredients:** 210 g wholewheat spaghetti; 400 g firm tofu, cubed; 350 g broccoli florets; 40 g peanut butter; 1½ tbsp soy sauce; 1 lime; 15 g ginger, grated; 1 tbsp oil.

**Directions:** Cook spaghetti, adding broccoli for the final 4 minutes. Mix peanut butter, soy sauce, ginger, lime juice and 100 ml pasta water. Brown dry tofu in oil for 8 minutes. Add noodles, broccoli and sauce and toss over gentle heat, loosening with water if necessary.

### 4. Red Lentil Dal with Spinach
**Ingredients:** 210 g red lentils, rinsed; 1 onion, chopped; 2 garlic cloves and 20 g ginger, minced; 1 tsp each cumin and turmeric; 750 ml vegetable stock; 150 g spinach; 150 g rice; ½ lemon; 1 tbsp oil; salt.

**Directions:** Cook rice. Soften onion in oil for 6 minutes, then add garlic, ginger and spices for 1 minute. Add lentils and stock and simmer for 20–25 minutes, stirring occasionally. Add water if thick, wilt in spinach and finish with lemon juice and salt. Serve with rice.

### 5. Black Bean and Pepper Chili
**Ingredients:** 2 cans black beans; 1 onion and 2 peppers, diced; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 1 can tomatoes; 150 g sweetcorn; 1 tsp each cumin and smoked paprika; ¼ tsp chili flakes; 150 g rice; 1 lime; 1 tbsp oil; salt.

**Directions:** Cook rice. Soften onion and peppers in oil for 7 minutes. Add garlic and spices, then tomatoes, beans, corn and 150 ml water. Simmer for 20 minutes, mashing a few beans to thicken. Finish with lime juice and salt; serve over rice.

### 6. Chickpea, Tomato and Coconut Curry
**Ingredients:** 2 cans chickpeas; 1 can tomatoes; 200 ml light coconut milk; 1 onion, chopped; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 1 tbsp curry powder; 200 g spinach; 150 g rice; ½ lime; 1 tbsp oil; salt.

**Directions:** Cook rice. Soften onion in oil, add garlic and curry powder for 1 minute, then chickpeas, tomatoes and coconut milk. Simmer for 15 minutes. Stir in spinach until wilted, add lime juice and salt, and serve with rice.

### 7. Aubergine and Lentil Tomato Bake
**Ingredients:** 2 aubergines, cubed; 2 cans lentils; 400 ml passata; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 1 tsp oregano; 1½ tbsp olive oil; 30 g breadcrumbs; 180 g wholegrain bread; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Toss aubergine with oil and salt; roast at 220°C for 25 minutes. Combine with lentils, passata, garlic, oregano and 100 ml water in a baking dish. Top with breadcrumbs and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes, until bubbling. Season and serve with bread.

### 8. Mushroom and Walnut Bolognese
**Ingredients:** 240 g spaghetti; 400 g mushrooms, finely chopped; 1 can lentils; 1 onion and 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 400 ml passata; 30 g walnuts, finely chopped; 1 tsp oregano; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Cook onion and mushrooms in oil for 10 minutes, until moisture mostly evaporates. Add garlic, lentils, walnuts, passata and oregano and simmer for 15 minutes. Cook spaghetti, toss with sauce and a splash of pasta water, and season.

### 9. Roasted Pepper and White Bean Orzo
**Ingredients:** 210 g orzo; 1 can cannellini beans; 250 g jarred roasted peppers, drained and sliced; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 550 ml vegetable stock; 150 g spinach; ½ lemon; 1 tbsp olive oil; pepper.

**Directions:** Gently cook garlic in oil, add orzo, beans, peppers and stock. Simmer uncovered for 10–15 minutes, stirring often and topping up with water as needed, until orzo is tender. Wilt in spinach and finish with lemon juice and pepper.

### 10. Satay Tofu Rice Bowls
**Ingredients:** 450 g firm tofu, cubed; 180 g rice; 1 cucumber, diced; 2 carrots, grated; 40 g peanut butter; 1 tbsp soy sauce; 1 lime; 1 tsp curry powder; 1 tbsp oil.

**Directions:** Cook rice. Mix peanut butter, soy sauce, curry powder, lime juice and 80 ml hot water. Pat tofu dry and brown in oil for 8–10 minutes. Add half the sauce and warm gently. Serve over rice with cucumber and carrot, spooning remaining sauce on top.

### 11. Sweet Potato and Chickpea Tray with Lemon Tahini
**Ingredients:** 500 g sweet potato, small cubes; 2 cans chickpeas; 1 red onion, wedges; 1 tsp cumin; 1½ tbsp olive oil; 35 g tahini; 1 lemon; 100 g rocket; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Toss sweet potato, chickpeas and onion with oil, cumin and salt; roast at 210°C for 30 minutes, turning once. Whisk tahini with lemon juice and enough water to make a pouring sauce. Serve vegetables over rocket, add sauce and pepper.

### 12. Chickpea, Herb and Cucumber Couscous
**Ingredients:** 180 g couscous; 1 can chickpeas; 1 cucumber and 3 tomatoes, diced; 25 g parsley, chopped; 10 g mint, chopped; 1 lemon; 1 tbsp olive oil; 30 g pumpkin seeds; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Prepare couscous according to its packet and fluff. Toss with chickpeas, vegetables and herbs. Add lemon juice, oil and seasoning. Scatter with pumpkin seeds and serve warm or cool.

### 13. Sweetcorn and White Bean Chowder
**Ingredients:** 2 cans cannellini beans; 250 g sweetcorn; 300 g potatoes, diced; 1 onion, chopped; 700 ml vegetable stock; 150 ml unsweetened soy milk; ½ tsp smoked paprika; 1 tbsp oil; 15 g chives, chopped; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Soften onion in oil; add paprika, potatoes and stock. Simmer for 15 minutes, until potatoes are tender. Add beans, corn and soy milk for 5 minutes. Blend one-third and return to thicken the soup; season and add chives.

### 14. Spiced Tomato and Red Lentil Soup
**Ingredients:** 200 g red lentils, rinsed; 1 can tomatoes; 1 onion and 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 1 tsp each cumin and smoked paprika; 800 ml vegetable stock; ½ lemon; 1 tbsp olive oil; 180 g wholegrain bread; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Soften onion in oil; add garlic and spices for 1 minute. Add lentils, tomatoes and stock and simmer for 20–25 minutes, until lentils soften. Blend smooth or leave chunky. Finish with lemon juice and seasoning; serve with bread.

### 15. Broccoli, White Bean and Mustard Soup
**Ingredients:** 500 g broccoli, florets and diced stalk; 2 cans cannellini beans; 1 onion and 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 750 ml vegetable stock; 2 tsp Dijon mustard; 1 tbsp olive oil; 180 g wholegrain bread; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Soften onion in oil, add garlic, broccoli stalks, beans and stock and simmer for 8 minutes. Add florets for 7–10 minutes. Blend smooth, stir in mustard and season. Serve with bread.

### 16. Lemon-Garlic Butter Beans and Greens
**Ingredients:** 2 cans butter beans; 250 g spinach; 300 g cherry tomatoes, halved; 3 garlic cloves, sliced; 150 ml vegetable stock; 1 lemon; 1 tbsp olive oil; 180 g wholegrain bread; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Gently cook garlic in oil for 1 minute. Add tomatoes for 5 minutes, then beans and stock and simmer for 8 minutes. Mash a few beans to thicken and wilt in spinach. Add lemon zest, juice and seasoning; serve with bread.

### 17. Spiced Chickpea Pitas with Yogurt Slaw
**Ingredients:** 2 cans chickpeas; 3 large wholegrain pitas; 250 g cabbage, finely sliced; 1 carrot, grated; 150 g plant-based yogurt; 1 lemon; 1 tsp each cumin and smoked paprika; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt.

**Directions:** Mix cabbage and carrot with half the yogurt, half the lemon juice and salt. Fry chickpeas in oil with spices for 5 minutes, then roughly crush. Warm pitas and fill with chickpeas and slaw. Finish with remaining yogurt and lemon juice.

### 18. Sweet Potato and Kidney Bean Enchiladas
**Ingredients:** 400 g sweet potato, small cubes; 1 can kidney beans; 6 small tortillas; 400 ml passata; 1 onion, chopped; 1 tsp each cumin and paprika; 100 g plant-based yogurt; 1 tbsp oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Boil sweet potato for 10 minutes and drain. Soften onion in oil, add spices, beans, potato and 100 ml passata and season. Fill and roll tortillas, place in a dish and cover with remaining passata mixed with 75 ml water. Bake at 200°C for 20 minutes; serve with yogurt.

### 19. Mushroom and Black Bean Burgers
**Ingredients:** 2 cans black beans; 250 g mushrooms, finely chopped; 1 onion, finely chopped; 60 g oats; 30 g flour; 1 tsp smoked paprika; 3 wholegrain buns; 1 tomato, sliced; 100 g lettuce; 100 g plant-based yogurt; 1 tbsp oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Cook onion and mushrooms in half the oil until dry, then cool. Pat beans dry, mash and mix with mushrooms, oats, flour, paprika and seasoning; rest for 10 minutes. Shape three patties and fry in remaining oil over medium heat for 5 minutes per side, until firm and hot throughout. Serve in buns with vegetables and yogurt.

### 20. Ginger Tofu and Cabbage Stir-Fry
**Ingredients:** 450 g firm tofu, thin strips; 400 g cabbage, finely sliced; 2 carrots, grated; 180 g rice; 20 g ginger and 2 garlic cloves, minced; 1½ tbsp soy sauce; 1 tbsp oil; ½ lime.

**Directions:** Cook rice. Brown dry tofu in oil for 8 minutes and remove. Stir-fry cabbage and carrots for 5 minutes, adding a splash of water. Add ginger and garlic for 1 minute, then tofu and soy sauce. Heat through, finish with lime juice and serve over rice.

### 21. Tofu and Pea Fried Rice
**Ingredients:** 400 g firm tofu, crumbled; 180 g rice; 250 g frozen peas; 1 carrot, finely diced; 3 spring onions, sliced; 1½ tbsp soy sauce; ½ tsp turmeric; 1 tbsp oil; ½ lime.

**Directions:** Cook rice and spread on a tray while preparing the rest. Fry carrot in oil for 4 minutes, add tofu and turmeric for 5 minutes, then peas for 3 minutes. Add rice, soy sauce and spring onions and stir-fry until everything is piping hot. Finish with lime juice and serve immediately.

### 22. Greek-Inspired Butter Bean Bake
**Ingredients:** 2 cans butter beans; 1 onion and 2 carrots, finely diced; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 1 can tomatoes; 1 tsp oregano; 20 g parsley, chopped; 1 tbsp olive oil; 180 g wholegrain bread; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Soften onion and carrots in oil for 8 minutes. Add garlic, tomatoes, oregano, beans and 150 ml water. Transfer to a baking dish and bake uncovered at 190°C for 30 minutes, until sauce thickens. Season, add parsley and serve with bread.

### 23. Pea, Basil and Walnut Pesto Pasta
**Ingredients:** 240 g pasta; 350 g frozen peas; 25 g basil; 35 g walnuts; 1 small garlic clove; 1 lemon; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Cook pasta, adding peas for the final 3 minutes, and reserve water. Blend basil, walnuts, garlic, oil, lemon juice, half the peas and 100 ml pasta water. Toss drained pasta and remaining peas with sauce off the heat, adding more water if needed. Season.

### 24. Lemon-Courgette and Chickpea Spaghetti
**Ingredients:** 240 g spaghetti; 2 courgettes, coarsely grated; 1 can chickpeas; 2 garlic cloves, sliced; 1 lemon; 15 g basil; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Cook spaghetti and reserve water. Cook courgettes in oil for 7 minutes; add garlic and chickpeas for 3 minutes. Toss with spaghetti, lemon zest and 75 ml pasta water. Add lemon juice to taste, torn basil and seasoning.

### 25. Edamame and Cucumber Rice Bowls
**Ingredients:** 180 g rice; 450 g frozen shelled edamame; 1 cucumber, diced; 2 carrots, grated; 1 avocado, sliced; 1½ tbsp soy sauce; 1 lime; 1 tsp sesame oil; 15 g sesame seeds.

**Directions:** Cook rice and edamame according to their packets. Mix soy sauce, lime juice and sesame oil. Divide rice among bowls and add edamame, cucumber, carrot and avocado. Spoon over dressing and scatter with sesame seeds.

### 26. Lentil, Beetroot and Walnut Salad
**Ingredients:** 2 cans lentils; 400 g cooked beetroot, cubed; 100 g rocket; 1 apple, diced; 35 g walnuts; 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar; 2 tsp Dijon mustard; 1 tbsp olive oil; 180 g wholegrain bread; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Whisk vinegar, mustard, oil and seasoning. Toss lentils, beetroot and apple with dressing. Fold in rocket, scatter with chopped walnuts and serve with bread.

### 27. Avocado and White Bean Rye Toasts
**Ingredients:** 2 cans cannellini beans; 1 avocado; 6 large slices rye bread; 300 g tomatoes, diced; 1 lemon; 1 small garlic clove, grated; ¼ tsp chili flakes; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Roughly mash beans and avocado with garlic, lemon juice and seasoning. Toss tomatoes with oil and chili. Toast bread, spread generously with bean mixture and spoon tomatoes on top.

### 28. Carrot, Ginger and Peanut Lentil Soup
**Ingredients:** 500 g carrots, sliced; 160 g red lentils, rinsed; 1 onion, chopped; 20 g ginger and 2 garlic cloves, minced; 35 g peanut butter; 900 ml vegetable stock; ½ lime; 1 tbsp oil; 180 g wholegrain bread; salt.

**Directions:** Soften onion in oil, add garlic and ginger, then carrots, lentils and stock. Simmer for 25 minutes, until carrots are soft. Blend with peanut butter, add lime juice and salt, and loosen with water as needed. Serve with bread.

### 29. Pumpkin and Black Bean Rice Bowls
**Ingredients:** 550 g Hokkaido pumpkin, deseeded and diced; 1 can black beans; 150 g rice; 1 red pepper, diced; 1 tsp smoked paprika; ½ tsp cumin; 1 lime; 120 g plant-based yogurt; 1 tbsp oil; salt.

**Directions:** Toss pumpkin and pepper with oil, spices and salt; roast at 210°C for 25–30 minutes. Cook rice and warm beans with a splash of water. Divide everything among bowls and add yogurt mixed with lime juice.

### 30. Roasted Curry Cauliflower and Chickpeas
**Ingredients:** 600 g cauliflower florets; 2 cans chickpeas; 1 tbsp curry powder; 1½ tbsp oil; 180 g couscous; 150 g plant-based yogurt; 1 lemon; 15 g coriander, chopped; salt.

**Directions:** Toss cauliflower and chickpeas with oil, curry powder and salt; roast at 210°C for 25–30 minutes. Prepare couscous. Mix yogurt with lemon zest and juice to taste. Serve roasted vegetables over couscous, add sauce and coriander.

### 31. Smoky Lentil-Stuffed Peppers
**Ingredients:** 3 large peppers, halved and deseeded; 2 cans lentils; 120 g rice; 1 onion, chopped; 400 ml passata; 1 tsp each smoked paprika and oregano; 1 tbsp olive oil; 120 g plant-based yogurt; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Cook rice. Soften onion in oil, add lentils, spices, rice and 150 ml passata and season. Fill pepper halves and place in a dish with remaining passata and 150 ml water around them. Cover and bake at 190°C for 30 minutes; uncover for 10 minutes. Serve with yogurt.

### 32. Mushroom, Pea and Lemon Risotto
**Ingredients:** 210 g risotto rice; 400 g mushrooms, sliced; 250 g frozen peas; 1 onion, chopped; 850 ml hot vegetable stock; ½ lemon; 25 g walnuts, chopped; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Brown mushrooms in oil and remove. Soften onion in the same pan with a splash of water, add rice, then stock gradually, stirring frequently for 18–22 minutes. Add peas and mushrooms for the final 4 minutes. When rice is tender, add lemon juice, seasoning and walnuts.

### 33. White Bean and Courgette Cakes
**Ingredients:** 2 cans cannellini beans; 1 large courgette, grated; 70 g flour; 30 g oats; 1 tsp cumin; 15 g parsley, chopped; 120 g plant-based yogurt; ½ lemon; 3 tomatoes, sliced; 1½ tbsp oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Salt courgette lightly, leave for 10 minutes and squeeze very dry. Pat beans dry and mash; mix with courgette, flour, oats, cumin, parsley and seasoning. Rest for 10 minutes and form nine small cakes. Fry in batches in a lightly oiled nonstick pan for 4 minutes per side, until firm and hot throughout. Serve with tomatoes and lemon yogurt.

### 34. Lemon Potato, Green Bean and Chickpea Pan
**Ingredients:** 550 g potatoes, 2 cm chunks; 350 g green beans, halved; 1 can chickpeas; 2 garlic cloves, chopped; 1 tsp oregano; 1 lemon; 1½ tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Boil potatoes until tender, adding green beans for the final 6–8 minutes. Drain well. Fry potatoes in oil for 6 minutes, turning gently; add garlic, oregano, chickpeas and green beans for 3 minutes. Finish with lemon juice and seasoning.

### 35. Tofu, Mango and Crunchy Slaw Wraps
**Ingredients:** 450 g firm tofu, strips; 3 large wholegrain wraps; 1 mango, diced; 250 g cabbage, shredded; 1 carrot, grated; 120 g plant-based yogurt; 1 lime; 1 tsp smoked paprika; 1 tbsp oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Pat tofu dry, season with paprika, salt and pepper and fry in oil for 8–10 minutes. Mix cabbage, carrot and mango with yogurt and lime juice. Warm wraps and fill with tofu and slaw; fold in the sides and roll.

## Latvian vegetarian dishes

### 1. Aukstā zupa — Cold Beetroot and Kefir Soup
**Ingredients:** 400 g cooked beetroot, grated; 750 ml plain kefir; 1 cucumber, diced; 3 eggs; 450 g potatoes, chunked; 3 spring onions, sliced; 15 g dill, chopped; 1 tsp mustard; 1 tbsp cider vinegar; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Boil eggs for 9–10 minutes, cool and chop. Boil potatoes until tender and let cool slightly. Mix beetroot, cucumber, kefir, spring onions, dill, mustard and vinegar; thin with cold water if needed and season. Chill for 30 minutes. Add eggs and serve with potatoes alongside. [^L1]

### 2. Bukstiņputra — Barley and Potato Porridge with Mushrooms
**Ingredients:** 150 g pearl barley, rinsed; 450 g potatoes, diced; 700 ml water; 150 ml milk; 300 g mushrooms, sliced; 1 onion, sliced; 1 tbsp oil; 90 g sour cream; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Simmer barley in water, covered, for 20 minutes. Add potatoes and cook another 20–25 minutes, until both are tender, adding water if needed. Stir in milk and mash roughly into thick porridge. Brown mushrooms and onion in oil for 10 minutes. Season and serve them over the porridge with sour cream. [^L2]

### 3. Kartupeļu pankūkas — Potato Pancakes with Dill Sour Cream
**Ingredients:** 750 g potatoes, peeled; 1 egg; 45 g flour; 2 tbsp oil; 150 g sour cream; 15 g dill, chopped; 1 cucumber, sliced; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Finely grate potatoes and squeeze out excess liquid. Mix with egg, flour and seasoning. Form nine thin pancakes and fry in batches in a lightly oiled pan over medium heat for 4–5 minutes per side, until golden and cooked inside. Mix sour cream with dill and serve with pancakes and cucumber. [^L3]

### 4. Sēņu mērce — Creamy Mushroom Sauce with New Potatoes
**Ingredients:** 500 g chanterelles or chestnut mushrooms, chopped; 1 onion, chopped; 150 ml cream; 150 ml vegetable stock; 1 tsp flour; 650 g potatoes; 15 g dill, chopped; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Boil potatoes until tender. Brown mushrooms and onion in oil for 10 minutes, until their liquid evaporates. Stir in flour, then stock and cream; simmer for 5–7 minutes, until slightly thickened. Season, add dill and serve over potatoes. [^L4]

### 5. Piena dārzeņu zupa — Milk and Summer Vegetable Soup
**Ingredients:** 350 g potatoes, diced; 2 carrots, sliced; 200 g cabbage, finely sliced; 200 g frozen peas; 500 ml water; 600 ml milk; 15 g dill, chopped; 180 g rye bread; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Simmer potatoes, carrots and cabbage in water for 12–15 minutes, until nearly tender; add water if required to keep vegetables submerged. Add peas and milk and simmer very gently for 4–5 minutes, avoiding a vigorous boil. Season, add dill and serve with bread. [^L5]

### 6. Pelēkie zirņi — Grey Peas with Onions and Smoked Tofu, Vegan Adaptation
**Ingredients:** 240 g dried Latvian grey peas or whole dried yellow peas; 250 g smoked tofu, diced; 2 onions, sliced; 1 tbsp oil; ½ tsp black pepper; salt.

**Directions:** Soak peas overnight, drain and cover generously with fresh water. Simmer for 60–90 minutes, or until tender; older peas may take longer. Soften onions in oil for 12 minutes, add tofu and brown for 5 minutes. Drain peas and toss with onions, tofu, pepper and salt. [^L6]

### 7. Puķukāposti ar brūnu sviestu — Cauliflower with Brown Butter, Side
**Ingredients:** 650 g cauliflower florets; 30 g butter; 30 g breadcrumbs; 25 g vegetarian hard cheese, grated; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Boil cauliflower for 5–7 minutes, until tender but firm, and drain thoroughly. Melt butter in a light-coloured pan over medium heat, swirling until golden-brown and nutty. Add breadcrumbs for 30–60 seconds, then immediately take off the heat. Spoon over cauliflower, add cheese and pepper, and serve as a side. [^L7]

### 8. Pupiņu salāti — Bean and Pickle Salad, Vegetarian Adaptation
**Ingredients:** 2 cans kidney beans; 1 red pepper, diced; 4 gherkins, diced; 3 hard-boiled eggs, chopped; 90 g sour cream; 30 g mayonnaise; 180 g rye bread; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Combine beans, pepper, gherkins and eggs. Mix sour cream and mayonnaise with seasoning and fold through. Chill for 20 minutes and serve with rye bread. [^L8]

### 9. Rasols — Potato, Pea and Pickle Salad, Vegetarian Adaptation
**Ingredients:** 500 g potatoes, diced; 2 carrots, diced; 200 g frozen peas; 3 eggs; 4 gherkins, diced; 100 g sour cream; 40 g mayonnaise; 1 tsp mustard; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Boil potatoes and carrots until tender, adding peas for the final 3 minutes; drain and cool. Boil eggs for 9–10 minutes, cool and dice. Combine with gherkins. Mix sour cream, mayonnaise and mustard, fold through and season. Chill for at least 30 minutes. [^L9]

### 10. Kāpostu tīteņi — Mushroom and Lentil Cabbage Rolls, Vegetarian Adaptation
**Ingredients:** 1 medium cabbage; 120 g rice; 1 can lentils; 250 g mushrooms, finely chopped; 1 onion and 2 carrots, finely chopped; 2 tbsp tomato paste; 150 g sour cream; 250 ml vegetable stock; 1 tbsp oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Cook rice. Core cabbage, lower into simmering water and lift off six large outer leaves as they soften; trim thick ribs. Cook mushrooms, half the onion and one carrot in oil until dry; mix with rice, lentils and seasoning. Fill leaves, fold in sides and roll; place seam-side down in a dish. Simmer remaining onion and carrot in stock for 8 minutes, stir in tomato paste and sour cream and pour over rolls. Cover and bake at 190°C for 40–50 minutes, until cabbage is tender. [^L10]

### 11. Saldie zirņi — Green Peas in Milk Sauce, Side
**Ingredients:** 500 g frozen peas; 15 g butter; 15 g flour; 200 ml milk; 10 g dill, chopped; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Boil peas for 3 minutes and drain. Melt butter, stir in flour for 1 minute, then gradually whisk in milk. Simmer gently for 3 minutes until smooth and lightly thickened. Add peas, heat through and finish with dill and seasoning. Serve as a side. [^L11]

### 12. Olu kultenis ar tomātiem — Tomato Scrambled Eggs
**Ingredients:** 6 eggs; 400 g tomatoes, chopped; 75 ml milk; 15 g butter; 15 g chives or dill, chopped; 180 g rye bread; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Cook tomatoes in butter over medium heat for 4–5 minutes, until juicy. Beat eggs with milk and seasoning. Add to the pan, lower the heat and stir gently until eggs are set to your liking. Finish with herbs and serve with rye bread. [^L12]

### 13. Ceptas siermaizītes — Baked Cheese-and-Egg Toasts, Light Meal
**Ingredients:** 6 large slices bread; 120 g Gouda, grated; 2 eggs; 10 g butter; 1 cucumber and 3 tomatoes, sliced; pepper.

**Directions:** Heat oven to 190°C. Lightly butter bread and place on a lined tray. Mix cheese, eggs and pepper and spread over bread. Bake for 12–15 minutes, until the topping is set and golden. Serve with cucumber and tomatoes. [^L13]

### 14. Sklandrauši — Carrot and Potato Rye Tarts, Snack
**Ingredients:** 180 g rye flour; 40 g butter, softened; approximately 90 ml warm water; 200 g potatoes, peeled and diced; 350 g carrots, diced; 40 ml milk; 60 g sour cream; 1 egg; 25 g sugar; ½ tsp caraway seeds; ¼ tsp salt.

**Directions:** Boil potatoes and carrots separately until tender. Mix flour, butter, water and salt into a firm, rollable dough; adjust water as needed. Roll 3 mm thick, cut six 10 cm rounds and turn up 1 cm rims. Mash potatoes with milk and place a thin layer in each case. Blend carrots with egg, half the sour cream and sugar; spoon over potatoes. Bake at 200°C for 25–30 minutes, until filling is set and pastry cooked. Spread remaining sour cream on top, add caraway and cool before serving two per person. [^L14]

### 15. Skābeņu zupa — Sorrel and Egg Soup, Vegetarian Adaptation
**Ingredients:** 450 g potatoes, diced; 1 onion, chopped; 200 g fresh sorrel, sliced, or 150 g jarred sorrel; 900 ml vegetable stock; 3 eggs; 90 g sour cream; 1 tbsp oil; 180 g rye bread; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Boil eggs for 9–10 minutes, cool and chop. Soften onion in oil, add potatoes and stock and simmer for 15 minutes, until tender. Add sorrel and simmer for 3–5 minutes. Stir in eggs, season and serve with sour cream and bread. If sorrel is unavailable, use 200 g spinach plus lemon juice to taste; this is a substitute, not the traditional flavour. [^L15]

### 16. Braised Cabbage and Leeks — Latvian Pīrāgi Filling Served as a Side
**Ingredients:** 650 g cabbage, finely sliced; 1 leek, washed and sliced; 250 ml vegetable stock; 20 g butter; 1 tbsp cider vinegar; 1 tsp potato starch or cornflour; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Soften leek and cabbage in butter for 8 minutes. Add stock and simmer partly covered for 25–30 minutes, until tender and most liquid has evaporated. Stir in vinegar. Mix starch with 1 tbsp cold water, add and simmer for 1 minute. Season and serve as a side, or cool before using as a pastry filling. [^L16]

## Latvian fish dishes

### 17. Zivs burkānu un tomātu mērcē — Fish in Carrot and Tomato Sauce
**Ingredients:** 500 g boneless cod or pollock fillets; 3 carrots, coarsely grated; 1 onion, sliced; 2 tbsp tomato paste; 300 ml vegetable stock; 1 tbsp cider vinegar; 1 tsp sugar; 1 tbsp oil; 500 g potatoes; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Boil potatoes until tender. Soften onion and carrots in oil for 8 minutes; add tomato paste for 1 minute, then stock, vinegar and sugar. Simmer for 10 minutes and season. Nestle fish into sauce, cover and simmer gently for 8–12 minutes, until cooked through. Serve warm with potatoes. [^L17]

### 18. Zivju zupa — Fish, Potato and Dill Soup
**Ingredients:** 450 g boneless white fish, cubed; 400 g potatoes, diced; 1 onion and 1 carrot, chopped; 800 ml fish or vegetable stock; 200 ml milk; 1 bay leaf; 20 g dill, chopped; 60 g sour cream; 150 g rye bread; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Simmer onion, carrot, potatoes and bay in stock for 15–20 minutes, until vegetables are tender. Add fish and milk and simmer very gently for 5–7 minutes, until fish is cooked through. Remove bay, season and add dill. Serve with sour cream and rye bread. [^L18]

### 19. Siļķe uz kartupeļu un ābolu plāceņa — Herring on Potato-Apple Cakes
**Ingredients:** 300 g ready-to-eat cured herring fillets, drained and sliced; 600 g potatoes, peeled; 1 apple, cored; 120 g sour cream; 15 g dill, chopped; 3 spring onions, sliced; 2 tbsp oil; pepper and a little salt.

**Directions:** Coarsely grate potatoes and apple and gently squeeze out excess liquid; do not rinse. Season lightly and form six thin cakes. Fry in batches in a lightly oiled nonstick pan over medium heat for 4–5 minutes per side, until golden and cooked inside. Top with herring, sour cream, dill and spring onions. [^L19]

### 20. Siļķe kažokā — Layered Herring, Beetroot and Apple Salad
**Ingredients:** 300 g ready-to-eat cured herring fillets, drained and diced; 400 g cooked beetroot, grated; 2 small apples, grated; 3 hard-boiled eggs, sliced; 150 g sour cream; 1 lemon; 3 spring onions, sliced; 150 g rye bread; pepper.

**Directions:** Toss beetroot with a little lemon zest and juice; toss apples with more lemon juice to prevent browning. Season sour cream with pepper. Layer beetroot, herring and apple in a shallow dish, spreading a little sour cream between layers. Top with remaining cream, eggs and spring onions. Chill for 1 hour and serve with bread. [^L20]

### 21. Zvejnieka brokastis — Sprat and Cheese Rye Toasts, Light Meal
**Ingredients:** 220 g canned smoked sprats, drained; 75 g Gouda, grated; ½ small onion, very finely chopped; 60 g sour cream; 6 slices rye bread, approximately 240 g; 1 cucumber, sliced; 1 lemon; pepper.

**Directions:** Roughly mash sprats with cheese, onion, sour cream and pepper. Toast rye bread and spread with the fish mixture. Add lemon juice to taste and serve with cucumber alongside. [^L21]

### 22. New Potato and Smoked Fish Salad with Herb Dressing
**Ingredients:** 500 g new potatoes, halved; 300 g hot-smoked mackerel or trout, skin and bones removed; 150 g radishes, sliced; 150 g frozen peas; 100 g mixed leaves; ½ red onion, sliced; 20 g dill and parsley, chopped; 25 g almonds; ½ lemon; 1½ tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

**Directions:** Boil potatoes until tender, adding peas for the final 3 minutes; drain and cool. Blend herbs, almonds, oil, lemon juice and 2–3 tbsp water into a dressing and season. Arrange leaves, potatoes, peas, radishes, onion and flaked fish on plates. Spoon dressing over and serve. [^L22]
